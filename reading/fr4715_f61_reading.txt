RUNNING TEXT
Nous avons parlé familierement et amplement de toutes choses avec [S], et des(?) particulierement sur les plus
importantes. Il seroit trop long vous en dire les propos, et combien [S] est capable a les entendre, et plein de
generosité pour beaucoup deservir. Mais on croit, comme je le croys aussi, que les choses sont [h] trop avancees,
et que si nous mesmes, en conoyssants le fruit, en fesions la premiere pointe, on ne differeroit point du reste.
Cependant on n'ose envoyer depardella, pour n'i mettre jalousie au beau pere maintenant qu'on en a affere. Vous
estes sur les lieux. Vous verres s'il s'en presentoit les occasions, et s'il s'en pouvoit prendre quelqu'une je ne
seroys pas paresseux, si le gros home l'entendoit tant soit peu.

TRANSLATION
We spoke familiarly and at length of all things with [S], and of some(?) in particular, of the most important.
It would take too long to tell you what was said, and how able [S] is to understand them, and how full of
generosity to do much service. But people believe, as I do also, that things are [h] too far advanced, and that if
we ourselves, knowing the fruit of it, made the first move, the rest would not be delayed. Meanwhile no one dares
to send [anyone] over there, so as not to give jealousy to the father-in-law now that we need him. You are on the
spot. You will see if occasions arise; and if one could be taken I would not be idle, if the fat man understood
it even a little.

WHAT IS NEW AGAINST THE EARLIER WORK
- Lines 10-11: the run reads "le gros home l'entendoit" (signs l/y, e/r, g/t, e/r, b/o, f/s, h/u, b/o, then
  m, e, l, e, n, t, e, n, d, o, i, t; "home" = homme, split over the line end). Tomokiyo read only line 11, as
  "me l'entendoit" (the pronoun "me"); cipher-lab's fragment for line 10 was "letresur", not a reading.
- Lines 1-2: after "avec" comes a person sign [S], then "et", a struck sign, and "des" (d at the end of line 1,
  a null and "es" on line 2). The same sign [S] is the subject of "est capable" in line 3.
- The reading is one sentence of connected French over the whole slip.

DOUBTS
- "et des particulierement": the signs give e/r g/t, then d/q, null, e/r f/s. "et des" is the only French
  reading, but the phrase is odd; the struck sign may hide a correction.
- "deservir" + the ring sign: the ring sign is a null inside "capable"; here it may be a null or stand for an
  object (a person or a cause).
- [h] in line 5: one occurrence; not read.
- "a les entendre": the "a" is taken as the clear word "à", not a null.
- Who is who. [S]: not identified (a person who is "capable" and "plein de generosité"). "le gros home": the
  duke of Mayenne was known for his bulk, and the cipher is his party's; this is a guess. "le beau pere":
  two candidates, both guesses: Philip II (father-in-law of the duke of Savoy from 1585), or the duke of
  Nevers (father-in-law of the duke of Longueville from 1588; the volume is Nevers's cipher file and the
  leaves beside f. 61 are letters to him of December 1589).
- Date: none on the slip. The neighbouring leaves are of 1589; Tomokiyo's dated letters in this cipher are of
  1592-93. The nulls set this hand apart from all of them.
