# Prior-art check in the volume: BnF fr. 3984, with fr. 3983, fr. 3985 and fr. 4715

Date of the check: 1 October 2026. Source: Gallica page images (microfilm) and the BnF catalogue.

## Verdict

**None found in the folios examined.** No contemporary decipherment of f. 186 (to Pietro Aldobrandini) or of
f. 189 (to Girolamo Frachetta) is bound in fr. 3984 ff. 150-274.

The result is stronger than "not found". The office that deciphered the sibling letters wrote, three times,
that it did **not** decipher these two:

1. **fr. 3984 f. 187v** (image 350), office endorsement on the address panel of the Aldobrandini letter:
   "Lre du Sr desportes du 22 Juillet au seigneur Pietro aldobrandin / **non encore deschiffree**", office number 13.
2. **fr. 3984 f. 189v** (image 354), office endorsement on the address panel of the Frachetta letter. The gutter
   hides the first line. The visible text: "[...] **encore deschiffree**. Et a notter que c'est le mesme chiffre que
   celle de sa Sainctete et de Mr de Lizieux", office number 14.
3. **fr. 3983 f. 141v** (Gallica `btv1b9059406b`, image 250), the office digest "Extraict de lres Interceptes tant
   en chiffre qu'aultrement" (ff. 140-144, endorsed on f. 145v "[Nevers?] 1593 sett. / Extraict de plusieurs lres
   Interceptes"):
   - entry 13: "C'est une semblable lre en chiffre dud. desportes au sieur Pietre Aldobrandin nepveu de sa
     sainctete **que lon n'a eu loisir encores de deschiffrer** Mais doibt estre de mesme subgect, Et est du mesme
     datte 22. Juillet 1593."
   - entry 14: "C'est encore une pareille lettre en mesme chiffre d'icelluy desportes au seigneur Hieronime
     Frachette **qui n'a non plus encore esté deschiffrée** Et est du mesme datte et de mesme chiffre que les trois
     precedentes."

The digest gives a summary for entries 10, 11 and 12 (the three deciphered letters). It gives no summary for
entries 13 and 14. The digest includes letters up to 22 August 1593, and its endorsement reads "1593 sett.".
So in September 1593 the two letters were still unread in the office.

Comparison with our draft (`tmp/read_f189.txt`): no page seen in any of the four volumes has the words "nostre
volonte estoit bonne la puissance asses grande" or "par la lettre de ... vous saures au vray lestat general de
nos affaires". The only French decipherments near the cipher letters are f. 175 (Talmet), ff. 177-178 (to the
Pope) and ff. 184-185 (to Lisieux). I read the first and last lines of each one. None is a text to Aldobrandini
or to Frachetta.

## Findings that change the dating, attribution or description of the letters

1. **The office numbers.** The numbers on the address panels (11 = to the Pope, 12 = to Lisieux, 13 = to
   Aldobrandini, 14 = to Frachetta, 10 = Lisieux to Desportes) are the entry numbers of the digest in fr. 3983
   ff. 140-144. The digest is the contemporary finding aid for the whole intercepted packet.
2. **The letter to the Pope has three cipher pages, not two.** It is f. 176r, f. 176v and **f. 179r** (19 more
   lines of cipher, the clear close and the signature). Its decipherment has three pages: f. 177r, f. 177v and
   **f. 178r** (13 lines). The start of f. 178r matches the last three gold lines of f. 176v ("leur conduite nous
   fait recognoistre par force un qui se veut tantost dire catolique ..."). So f. 178r gives the plain text for
   the 19 cipher lines of f. 179r. That is about 19 new aligned lines for training and for tests. The catalogue
   and the project notes give the item as ff. 176-177 only.
3. **The intercepted letter to the Pope is a duplicate.** f. 179r has the sender's note "Duplicata de la mesme
   depesche dud. xxije Juillet". A first copy went by another route. The same can be true of the letters to
   Aldobrandini and Frachetta, but those two carry no such note.
4. **Date.** Both unread letters carry their date in clear at the end: "De Paris ce xxije Juillet 1593"
   (f. 186v and f. 189r). The date "22 de Juillet 1593" at the head of each first page is a later archivist's
   hand. The date 22 July is confirmed.
5. **Attribution of f. 189.** The letter to Frachetta is not signed with the name. It closes with a short formula
   and a paraph. The attribution to Desportes rests on the office endorsement, on the digest ("d'icelluy
   desportes"), on the hand and on the cipher. The letter to Aldobrandini is signed "Desportes" in full.
6. **Same cipher: the office said so.** f. 189v: "c'est le mesme chiffre que celle de sa Sainctete et de Mr de
   Lizieux". The digest: "de mesme chiffre que les trois precedentes". The office had the key from the letters
   that it had read, and it did not use it on these two for lack of time ("que lon n'a eu loisir").
7. **Who was to read the letters in Rome.** In the letter to Lisieux (decipherment, f. 184v) Desportes asks:
   "faire bien deschiffrer toutes les despesches & que le tout soit representé a sa Saincteté & au Sr dom
   Piettre". So the bishop of Lisieux held the key and deciphered the letters for the Pope and for Aldobrandini.
   In f. 274r Lisieux writes: "Aussy tost que j'eu receu le pacquet j'envoiay au seigr Pietro celles de sa Steté
   et les siennes" and "J'ay esté l'interprete de celles que vous escrivez au Sr Hieronimo qui est si peu content
   de ce quelles contiennent quil ne se peut persuader quil soit vray". "Sr Hieronimo" is Frachetta. So Frachetta
   did not hold the key either: Lisieux read Desportes' letters to him. The letters to Aldobrandini and Frachetta
   are best described as sent "through the bishop of Lisieux".
8. **The name and the title.** The office writes "desportes", "Baudouyn desportes" and "desportes Baudouyn". The
   signature is "Desportes". The address on f. 274v gives his title: "Monsieur desportes Sr de beuvillier
   secretaire de la maison et couronne de france". The printed truce (f. 267v) is countersigned "Baudouin".
9. **The interception.** The endorsement on f. 181v says that the oath of 23 July was "trouvé dans un paquet en
   chiffre dud. Sr legat avec ses lres du 26 Juillet 93". The Desportes letters (22 July, postscript 26 July), the
   Sega letters (15 and 26 July), Pellevé (27 July) and the Lyon letters (24 and 28 July) are in one numbered
   series. They come from one capture, or from captures a few days apart, after 28 July 1593.
10. **A possible catalogue slip.** f. 175 (cat. no. 83, "déchiffrement de la lettre n° 74", Talmet, 20 July) seems
    to end "de Paris ce xxije Juillet". I did not compare it with f. 153, and my reading of that date is not
    checked. It does not concern Desportes.

## Doubts

- The first line of the endorsement on f. 189v is in the gutter of the microfilm. I read "[...] encore
  deschiffree" and I supply "non" from f. 187v and from the digest.
- The first word of the endorsement on fr. 3983 f. 145v ("Nevers"?) and the last line of the endorsement on
  fr. 3984 f. 181v are doubtful.
- Readings of the secretary hand in the quotations are mine, made from the images. Abbreviations are expanded
  without marks.
- The contact sheets (700 px) show the type of each page. They do not let me read each page in full. A short
  French note of a few lines on one of those pages can escape this check. A full-page decipherment cannot.
