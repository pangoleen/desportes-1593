# BnF ms. francais 3984, f. 177r, f. 177v, f. 178r: the 1593 decipherment of f. 176r-v and f. 179r
# (Desportes to Clement VIII, Paris, 22 July 1593). Gallica btv1b9060633d, images 329, 330, 331.
# Second reading by Claude, 3 Oct 2026, from native crops of canvases c329, c330, c331, line by line, with the
# cipher text (gold/f176r.txt, f176v.txt, f179r.txt) beside it. It replaces the first reading of 1 Oct 2026
# (f177_office_decipherment_1593.txt), which has many errors. No palaeographer has checked this file.
# One manuscript line per line. r = f. 177r, v = f. 177v, w = f. 178r.
# Abbreviations are expanded without marks (q = que, ql = quil, por = pour, nre = nostre, fe = faire, aue = aultre,
# cath. = catholique, lres = lettres). No apostrophes. Kept as written, because they render cipher signs:
# "R. dhespaigne", "R. de N.", "V. S.", "Mr", "Mons", and figures.
# (?) after a word = doubtful reading. [sign] = a cipher sign that the clerk drew into the text.
# <struck: ...> = words struck out by the clerk. ^word^ = word added above the line.
# Underlines and a marginal "Nota" brace (r22-r34, v15-v20) are by a reader; they are not marked here.
# Hands: A = secretary hand with the q-stroke sign for "que". B = more angular secretary hand, "que" and "Et" in full.
#
