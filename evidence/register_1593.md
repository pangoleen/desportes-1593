# The 1593 register: the office did not decipher these two letters

The letters were intercepted by the royalist side and are bound in the papers of the Duke of Nevers. The office
drew up a list, "Extraict de lectres interceptés, tant en chiffre qu'aultrement" (Paris, BnF, ms. français 3983,
ff. 140-146). Entries 10 to 14 are the packet of 22 July 1593.

## Entries 13 and 14 (f. 141v)

Gallica: ark btv1b9059406b, image 250. Image: [../images/fr3983_f141v_entries_13_14.jpg](../images/fr3983_f141v_entries_13_14.jpg).

Entry 13:

> C'est une semblable lettre en chiffre dudit Desportes au sieur Pietre Aldobrandin, nepveu de Sa Saincteté, que
> l'on n'a eu loisir encores de deschiffrer. Mais doibt estre de mesme subgect, et est du mesme datte, 22 juillet 1593.

"This is a similar letter in cipher from the said Desportes to signor Pietro Aldobrandini, nephew of His Holiness,
which we have not yet had the leisure to decipher. But it must be on the same subject, and it has the same date,
22 July 1593."

Entry 14:

> C'est encore une pareille lettre en mesme chiffre d'icelluy Desportes au seigneur Hieronime Frachette, qui n'a non
> plus encore esté deschiffrée. Et est du mesme datte et de mesme chiffre que les trois precedentes.

"This is again a like letter in the same cipher, from the same Desportes to signor Girolamo Frachetta, which has
not yet been deciphered either. It has the same date and the same cipher as the three before it."

## The endorsement on the letter itself (ms. français 3984, f. 189v)

> [...] encore deschiffrée. Est à notter que c'est le mesme chiffre que celle de Sa Saincteté et de Mr de Lizieux.
> 14

"[...] yet deciphered. Note that it is the same cipher as that of His Holiness and of Monsieur de Lisieux. 14"

The first words of the note are in the gutter of the image. The number 14 matches the register entry.

## What this shows

- The office deciphered three letters of the packet (to the Pope and to the bishop of Lisieux) and set two aside.
- The BnF catalogue agrees: items 84 and 87 of ms. français 3984 are "avec chiffre et déchiffrement"; items 88 and
  90, the two letters read here, are "avec chiffre" only.
- No decipherment of either letter was found in ms. français 3983, 3984 or 3985.
