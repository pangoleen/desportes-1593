# Notes
# r09 "poquinteste": Desportes wrote this name in clear text on f. 188r l. 10; the clerk copied it. The same
#     word is in cipher in f. 189 l. 17 (classes C B + D H I A G E F G E).
# r31 "Mons[ieu]r de S[ain]t Eustache": the cure of Saint-Eustache, Rene Benoist. "Le Perron": Jacques Davy du Perron.
# v02 the line starts with a blank of about one third of the width after "Monsieur": a name left out.
# v04 "en coclaiz ou nivelle": not understood. Perhaps an allusion to the proverb of Jean de Nivelle.
